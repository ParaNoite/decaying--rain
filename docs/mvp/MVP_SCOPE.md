# MVP Scope

The MVP validates whether the core loop works before expanding content.

## Must Include

- 1 fixed base map.
- 1 external scavenging area.
- 5 rain waves.
- 4 firearms: pistol, shotgun, SMG, rifle.
- 3 melee options: empty hands, balanced tool, heavy axe.
- 4 enemies: ruptured, doorbreaker, armored scavenger, wet gunner.
- 1 profession prototype: deserter.
- 2 random events: contaminated supply, faction war.
- Hunger.
- Status system interface stubs.
- Lightweight meta unlock placeholders.

## Out Of Scope

- Multiplayer.
- Large open world.
- Deep building system.
- Full trap system.
- Complex NPC dialogue.
- Full true-ending chain.
- Boss system.
- Permanent stat growth.

## Success Criteria

- Players understand the loop within 3 minutes.
- Daylight creates "push deeper or return" pressure.
- Rain defense uses melee and block frequently.
- Firearms feel valuable because they are scarce.
- Base breach creates recoverable panic, not instant failure.
- Death reasons are readable.
- Randomness creates replay interest without unwinnable runs.
